BUILT AROUND COLLABORATION

Bring your people. Build something.

Some parts are already working in our private pilot. Others are still on the workbench. Here’s a look at where it’s going.

◎

Teams

Get a crew together, invite people in and give your projects a home. Teams, invitations and project spaces are in the private pilot.

◌

Conversations

Talk things through, share progress and meet other builders. Team Chat and community rooms are still being developed.

⑂

Git Collaboration

Bring your code along. The private pilot supports team repositories, branches, comparisons and reviewed changes.

▤

The Archive

Good work should not disappear in a chat history. The Archive is growing, with review and publication tools still in progress.

THE BIG IDEA

A conversation can become something real.

An idea becomes a conversation. A few people form a Team. Then there's a project, a repository and a record of the work. The Archive helps keep the useful bits.

No popularity contest. No pressure to be an expert on day one. Just space to learn, contribute and build together.
