THE FEDERATION · PRIVATE DEVELOPMENT

Contingency

A place for people to build together.

Contingency is taking shape around a simple idea: people should have a welcoming space to connect, collaborate, build projects and demonstrate their work.

Explore the vision ↓

BUILT AROUND COLLABORATION

People, conversations and real work.

The private pilot already brings several building blocks together. Other parts are still being refined before a public launch.

◎

Teams

Create a shared home for collaborators, projects and participation. Team invitations, memberships and project spaces are already part of the private pilot.

◌

Conversations

Teams Chat and community rooms are central to the vision: places to coordinate work, exchange ideas and find collaborators. These experiences are still being developed.

⑂

Git Collaboration

Team repositories bring code and project history into the workspace, with branches, comparisons and reviewed changes in the private pilot.

▤

The Archive

A growing home for guides, knowledge and contributions. Its review and publication processes are still being prepared.

WHY IT MATTERS

More than another chat platform.

Conversation can lead to a team. A team can build a project. Git records how that work develops, while the Archive gives useful knowledge somewhere to live.

Contingency aims to connect those experiences without treating people as scores or promising opportunities that do not yet exist.

COMING TOGETHER, CAREFULLY

Not open to the public yet.

Contingency is currently a privately tested development platform. Public registration, paid opportunities and general access have not launched. We’ll share more as the Federation becomes ready.
