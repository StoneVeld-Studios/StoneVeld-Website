The Builder's Field Kit

A simple checklist for planning a website or digital project, before anyone writes code.

  • What problem or opportunity should this project address?
  • Who will use it, and what should they be able to do?
  • Which pages, tools, assets or information already exist?
  • What is essential for version one, and what can wait?
  • How will we test whether it works on mobile and desktop?
  • Who will maintain it once the first version is delivered?
