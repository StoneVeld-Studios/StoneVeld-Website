04 / HOW I APPROACH A PROBLEM

The method behind
the build.

Useful software rarely starts with typing. It starts with understanding what should happen—and what could go wrong.

  1. ◎01

    Understand

    Ask the questions. Find the real requirement, not just the symptom.

  2. ⌕02

    Investigate

    Inspect the evidence. Test likely causes and challenge assumptions.

  3. ◇03

    Build

    Choose a practical approach. Implement carefully and keep it understandable.

  4. ✓04

    Verify

    Test the result, document what changed and improve what the tests reveal.

Working principle: A successful test is evidence. It isn't permission to stop thinking.
