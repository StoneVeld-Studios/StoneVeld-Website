# PHOENIX-001 — Character Design Reference
**Production:** The Phoenix Will Rise Again — Chapter II: The Courage to Rise
**Status:** Visual design specification — awaiting Bianca's approval of the illustrated reference sheet.
**Source reference:** [Bianca's first illustrated Phoenix story](../../assets/Phoenix_story_product_.jpeg). This is the primary, authoritative model. The [Phoenix Rise extra artwork](../../assets/Bianca_phoenix_rise_extra.jpeg) is supplementary themed artwork, **not** a character redesign.
**Originals:** Do not overwrite or alter any source art or film.

## Visual identity, anchored in the first story
- A *young phoenix*, compact body with believable bird anatomy, not a giant adult dragon-bird.
- Soft rounded expressive head, large alert dark amber eyes, short curved yellow/golden hooked beak, upward fiery swept crest.
- Dense layered copper-red and burnt-orange feathers with orange-gold edges, warm amber highlights under the chest and throat.
- Wings broad and fully feathered: long spread primary feathers create a bold birdlike silhouette; flight pose must remain anatomically plausible.
- Tail has long elegant narrow tapering orange-red feathers that trail naturally in flight, with luminous golden tips.
- Talons birdlike and dark; perching profile has upright posture and head slightly larger relative to torso, reflecting youth.
- **No** human face, clothing, jewelry, metal armor, facial tattoos, antlers, extra tails or second pair of wings.
- Warm orange/gold flame-like lighting originates from feather edges and subtle embers, NOT an always-burning body. The bird can be wet, exhausted or dim without changing anatomical identity.

## Three approved-in-principle appearance states — same bird
1. **Dream / flight:** rich golden-orange feathers, youthful round face, clean wings and swept crest, hopeful eyes.
2. **Storm / fall:** wet, matted, darkened feathers; same crest/head/beak/tail count and body proportions, subdued glow; no gore.
3. **Awakening / rise:** same feathers become dry and gradually relit along their tips; wing silhouette remains identical; stronger golden rim-light but **no sudden giant adult mutation**.

## Reference sheet deliverables
A single **image-only** comparison sheet: full-body perching 3/4, full wingspan flight front/three-quarter, side profile, head close-up, folded-wing detail, and small muted-to-relit progression. Neutral light warm-grey / ivory background for true feather colour assessment. Do not add lettering into the illustration itself — the document provides the labels.

**Accuracy criterion:** The reference should be recognisable beside Bianca's original young phoenix in panels 1 and 2. It may simplify fine feather texture but must not invent a new phoenix identity.

## Scene layout colours
- Main feather: burnt copper / orange-red.
- Secondary feathers: deep rust and rich amber.
- Glowing edges: sunrise gold.
- Eye: dark brown/amber with small white highlight.
- Storm shadows: slate blue / near-charcoal **lighting only**.
- Butterfly cameo: violet and turquoise detail; never physically transforms the phoenix.

## Open decisions (Bianca)

- Whether flight tail trails as three or more distinct long feathers in final turnarounds, determined by her favourite original scene.
- Which of the generated reference poses best matches her original.
